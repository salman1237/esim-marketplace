3. Finding and buying an eSIM
-----------------------------

Open a destination to see every package for that country. Filter by data
allowance and by number of days, and enter a discount code if you have one —
it is applied when you buy, and only one code applies per order.

[screenshot: screenshots/03-explore-thailand.png — Thailand packages]

Each card shows the data, the validity and the price in your chosen currency,
along with the payment methods available.

**Press Buy Now.** What happens next depends on your balance:

- Enough in your wallet — the eSIM is bought immediately from your
  balance. Reward credit is always spent before your own money.
- Not enough — you are taken to the gateway (card, bKash, Nagad or
  Rocket) to pay for that order directly.

Within seconds you receive:

- the eSIM in your account under My eSIMs,
- an email containing the QR code, the activation details and a tracking
  link.

> Validity starts when you first install the eSIM, not when you buy it.
> Buying a week before departure does not shorten the plan.

If the supplier cannot deliver, the order is marked Failed and your money
is refunded automatically — back into exactly the balances you paid from.

4. The wallet
-------------

Wallet shows your balances, lets you top up and lists every transaction.

[screenshot: screenshots/10-customer-wallet.png — Wallet]


The three balances

| Balance | What it is | Can you spend it? | Can you withdraw it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main balance | Money you topped up, refunds, transferred commission | Yes | Yes |
| Reward credit | Referral rewards | Yes, on eSIMs | No, never |
| Commission earned | Merchant or agent earnings | Move it to your main balance first | Only after moving it |


Topping up

Type the amount in your own currency — the box follows whatever currency
your account is set to. The minimum and maximum are shown converted, so there
is no arithmetic to do.

Two routes:

- Pay via ShurjoPay — card, bKash, Nagad or Rocket, credited
  automatically when the payment confirms. If your currency is taka you are
  charged exactly the taka shown on the button.
- Manual deposit — send money to the published bKash, Nagad or bank
  account, then submit the transaction ID. Staff check it and credit your
  wallet. The screen tells you the amount to send and what will land in your
  wallet.

Every credit and debit appears in the ledger below, with the balance before
and after.

5. Your eSIMs and live status
-----------------------------

My eSIMs lists everything you have bought.

[screenshot: screenshots/11-customer-my-esims.png — My eSIMs]

Open one for the full live status page:

[screenshot: screenshots/12-customer-esim-live-status.png — Live eSIM status]

This page shows real figures from the carrier, not estimates:

| Panel | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Data used | Actual consumption, with the remainder of your allowance |
| SIM status | Active, installed or not yet installed, and the handset detected |
| Validity | Days, and the exact expiry — counted from first activation |
| Install on another device | One-tap iPhone and Android setup, the SM-DP+ address, the activation code and the full LPA string, each copyable |
| eSIM details | Plan, order reference, ICCID, purchase, activation and expiry times |
| Daily usage | Per-day breakdown where the carrier reports it |

Refresh fetches fresh figures from the carrier. Status is cached for five
minutes and a forced refresh is limited to once every 45 seconds, so the
button is never dead just because you looked at the page.

Print produces a printable sheet with the QR — useful if you are
installing on a phone that is not the one you are reading this on.

6. Sharing a tracking link
--------------------------

Every eSIM has a tracking link at the bottom of its status page. It opens
the same usage and installation information without anyone needing to sign in.

[screenshot: screenshots/18-public-tracking-link.png — Public tracking link]

Use it when the person travelling is not the person who bought the eSIM. The
link is also included in the delivery email.

The address contains a long random token, not a sequential number, so it
cannot be guessed and nobody can step from your eSIM to anyone else's. It
still shows real usage data, so share it only with whoever is actually using
the eSIM.

7. Referrals and discount codes
-------------------------------

Refer shows your personal referral code, a link to share, and what you
have earned.

[screenshot: screenshots/14-customer-refer.png — Referrals]

Rewards are configured by the platform and can be paid on three events:
someone joining with your code, their first purchase, and their becoming a
merchant. Rewards land in your reward credit balance — spendable on eSIMs,
never withdrawable.

Discount codes are entered on the destination page before buying. One code
applies per order.

8. Identity verification (KYC)
------------------------------

Your Profile holds your details, your preferred currency and language, and
your identity verification.

[screenshot: screenshots/15-customer-profile.png — Profile and KYC]

Submit your NID number and photographs of the document. Staff review it and
you are notified of the outcome. **You must have approved KYC before you can
withdraw any money.** Your documents are stored privately and are visible only
to authorised staff — never through a public address.

9. Withdrawing money
--------------------

From the wallet, choose Withdraw.

[screenshot: screenshots/13-customer-withdraw.png — Withdrawal request]

Before you can request one you need:

1. approved KYC, and
2. a saved payout account — a bank account or a mobile wallet such as
   bKash or Nagad.

What happens then:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| Requested | The amount leaves your main balance straight away and is held, so it cannot be spent twice |
| Approved / Paid | Staff send the money and upload proof. The conversion rate is frozen at this moment, so what you see is what you get |
| Rejected | The full amount is returned to your balance with a reason |
| Cancelled | You can cancel while it is still pending; the money comes straight back |

You and the platform both receive an email at every stage, showing the amount,
the fee, the rate used and the payout total.

Note: reward credit can never be withdrawn, and commission has to be moved
into your main balance first.

10. Support
-----------

Support lists your tickets.

[screenshot: screenshots/16-customer-support-list.png — Support tickets]

Open one to read the conversation and reply. You can attach up to five files
of 5 MB each — a screenshot of the error is usually the fastest route to an
answer.

[screenshot: screenshots/17-customer-support-thread.png — Support thread]

The thread and your email stay in step: when staff reply you get an email, and
you can carry on in either place. If you reply to a ticket that was already
resolved, it reopens automatically.

12. The agent portal
--------------------

Approved agents get an Agent Portal.

[screenshot: screenshots/25-agent-overview.png — Agent overview]

The overview shows how many partners you have, what they have sold, and what
you have earned — plus your invite link. Send that link to a travel
agency and their B2B application opens with your agent code already filled in,
so every eSIM they sell earns you commission from the first sale.


Your partners

[screenshot: screenshots/26-agent-merchants.png — Agent partners]

Here you can:

- see each partner's status, delivered sales, volume and what you have
  earned from them;
- add an existing merchant who does not already have an agent;
- set a bonus of 0–10% for a partner.

The bonus is taken from that partner's commission and paid to you **on top of
your own percentage**. It does nothing until an admin approves it — pending
requests are listed underneath with their status.


Sales, commission and statements

[screenshot: screenshots/27-agent-sales.png — Agent sales]

[screenshot: screenshots/28-agent-commission.png — Agent commission]

[screenshot: screenshots/29-agent-statement.png — Agent statement]

Commission is paid when an eSIM is delivered. A refunded order pays neither
you nor the partner.
